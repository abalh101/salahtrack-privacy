1. Data processing principles

SalahTrack is designed primarily as a local prayer app. There is currently no SalahTrack user account and no SalahTrack backend to which prayer or Ramadan history is uploaded. A large part of the app data is stored only on the device.

For certain functions, especially prayer-time calculation and location search, data may be transmitted to external services as described below.

2. Location data

If automatic location is enabled, SalahTrack may use an approximate or precise location to provide prayer times and the Qibla direction. Location access is optional; a location can also be selected manually. Coordinates, city, country, time zone and the selected location mode may be stored locally. The app does not request permanent background location access in the currently described version.

3. Prayer times and AlAdhan API

For prayer-time calculation or retrieval, SalahTrack uses AlAdhan via api.aladhan.com. Latitude and longitude and calculation parameters such as month, year, calculation method, Asr school and high-latitude rules may be transmitted over HTTPS. Network providers and the service necessarily receive connection data such as the IP address. SalahTrack does not transmit a SalahTrack user ID, advertising ID or self-generated device ID in the currently described implementation.

4. Geocoding and manual location search

When a location is searched manually, suggestions are requested, or coordinates are converted into a place name, place information or coordinates may be processed by geocoding services. Depending on the device and implementation, platform or system providers such as Apple or Google may be involved.

5. Locally stored data

Depending on use, prayer times and cached schedules, prayer status and confirmation/reminder times, Ramadan activities, location and calculation settings, language, design and notification settings, and other local app state may be stored on the device. Prayer and Ramadan history may reveal religious practice and is not uploaded to a SalahTrack backend in the currently described implementation.

6. Qibla and compass

The Qibla direction is calculated using location data. Compass readings may be used for the on-screen direction and are not permanently stored or transmitted to a SalahTrack backend in the currently described implementation.

7. Notifications

SalahTrack can schedule local notifications for prayers, reminders, snooze, Friday prayer and Ramadan functions. Notification data is managed locally by the app and operating system. The currently described version does not use a SalahTrack remote-push server or push-token infrastructure.

8. Analytics, advertising and tracking

The currently described version does not use advertising, tracking, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs such as Google Mobile Ads, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Meta, AppsFlyer, Adjust, Amplitude, Mixpanel or Sentry, and does not use an advertising ID to track users.

9. Report a problem and email support

The “Report a problem” feature lets users choose a category, describe a problem and optionally provide reproduction steps. SalahTrack prepares non-sensitive technical information such as app version, build number, platform, operating-system version, device model and app language. This information is shown before sending. Nothing is sent automatically: the user's email app is opened with a prepared report, which can be reviewed, edited, cancelled or explicitly sent by the user. Exact GPS location, prayer or Ramadan history, personal religious activity, advertising IDs and unique device identifiers are not automatically attached. If sent, the report is processed through the user's email provider and delivered to ar830222@gmail.com; that provider's privacy terms also apply.

10. In-app reviews

SalahTrack may open the Apple or Google system interface for an app review. Normal store services may be contacted; SalahTrack does not directly receive the content of the review through this feature.

11. Storage and deletion

Locally stored prayer and Ramadan data has no general automatic retention period in the currently described version. Local app data can be removed by deleting the app or, where supported by the operating system, clearing its app data.

12. Data security

Prayer-time network requests to AlAdhan use HTTPS. Local data is stored in the operating system's app sandbox. The currently described implementation does not add application-level encryption to SQLite or SharedPreferences.

13. Permissions

SalahTrack requests permissions needed for enabled features, particularly location and notifications. Permissions can be revoked in iOS or Android system settings; some features may then be limited.

14. Your data protection rights

Where applicable under data-protection law, you may have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, as well as the right to withdraw consent for the future. Privacy requests can be sent to ar830222@gmail.com. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority.

15. Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated when SalahTrack's functions, services, permissions or data flows change. The current version is published on this page.
